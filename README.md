# 🚗 Auto Care IMS

**Auto Care IMS** is a full-stack **Inventory Management System** designed for auto care businesses to efficiently manage vehicle parts, vendors, stock, transactions, and inventory operations.

The system uses a **.NET backend with RESTful APIs** and a modern **React frontend**, providing a structured solution for managing inventory while maintaining a simple and user-friendly interface.

## ✨ Features

* 📦 **Parts Management**
  Add, update, view, and manage vehicle parts and their details.

* 🏪 **Vendor Management**
  Maintain vendor information and manage supplier-related records.

* 📊 **Inventory Management**
  Monitor available stock and maintain accurate inventory records.

* 🔄 **Stock Transactions**
  Record inventory movements and track stock-related transactions.

* 🔍 **Search & Filtering**
  Easily find parts and inventory records using search and filtering functionality.

* 📋 **Inventory Records**
  Maintain organized records of stock and business operations.

* 🧾 **Transaction Management**
  Manage incoming and outgoing inventory transactions.

* 📱 **Responsive User Interface**
  React-based frontend designed for a smooth experience across different screen sizes.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of Auto Care IMS are to:

* Simplify inventory management for auto care businesses.
* Maintain accurate vehicle-part stock records.
* Manage vendors and supplier information efficiently.
* Track inventory transactions.
* Reduce manual inventory management.
* Provide a centralized system for managing business inventory.
* Demonstrate full-stack development using **C#, .NET, REST APIs, React, and SQL**.
